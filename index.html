        About

        Flightroute shows where you are on a map at 35,000 feet, with the phone in flight mode. Before you fly, enter your flight number or the two airports and it saves detailed street maps around both ends and an overview map along the route. In the air it uses your phone's own GPS, so there's no Wi-Fi or data needed.

        Features

        • Flight number lookup and a built-in list of 3,200 airports
        • Offline maps from OpenStreetMap, saved on your phone
        • Flight timer that starts at takeoff and stops on landing
        • Speed, altitude, distance to go and an estimated arrival time, in metric or imperial
        • Replay your saved flights, and reuse a saved or imported track as the route next time
        • Record your track and export it as GPX
        • Try a simulated flight before you go

        Tips for the flight

        • Open Flightroute at the gate before switching to flight mode, and wait for GPS good. The phone downloads satellite data while it has a connection, so it finds you much faster in the air.
        • Sit by a window and keep the phone near it: the aircraft's body blocks satellite signals.
        • Turn on Precise Location in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
        • Keep the phone plugged in and Flightroute on screen: iPhones pause GPS for web apps when the screen locks.

        Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, © OpenMapTiles, served by OpenFreeMap. Your position and tracks never leave your device.

        Add to Home Screen

        Apple doesn't let websites install themselves, so it takes three taps in Safari:

        1. 1Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar. If you can't see it, tap ⋯ first.
        2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
        3. 3Tap Add. Flightroute appears on your home screen; open it from there.

        Open it from the home screen from now on: saved maps are kept there, and it opens straight to the map.
